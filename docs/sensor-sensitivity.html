Which signals change a warning?

When Sentinel issues a maintenance warning, which input channels actually influence its score? We reran the same frozen model with one 30-cycle sensor or operating-setting channel replaced by its training-set mean, then measured the change in predicted risk. Below are recorded outcomes and reproducible, noncausal perturbation results.

What this does NOT prove: Changing a model input is not repairing a turbine or engine. Training-mean replacement can create physically unrealistic sensor combinations. A large score change is evidence of model sensitivity—not proof of a mechanical cause, safety readiness, or a trustworthy natural-language fault diagnosis.

Provenance & limitations

Model evidence, not storytelling

  • Frozen reconstruction: the checkpoint and original 100 official endpoint predictions reproduce before any perturbation is scored.
  • Selected examples: six previously recorded real cases must reproduce at their exact cycle and score before their sensitivities are included.
  • Counterfactual input: only one channel changes; the model is not retrained and the original threshold remains 0.5.
  • OpenTime distinction: this independent compact temporal model is not the organizer's genuine OpenTime 5B model.
