From telemetry to an auditable warning

The baseline pipeline is real and reproducible. The architecture is designed to be extended with time-series language-model inference when its model-specific components are available.

01 / INGEST

Observe past signals

Use a 30-cycle history of 21 sensor streams plus three engine operating settings, never future telemetry.

02 / REASON

Estimate remaining life

A gradient-boosted baseline and an original 12-epoch temporal QA Transformer produce held-out remaining-life estimates and 30-cycle warning responses.

03 / DECIDE

Surface an actionable warning

Flag engines estimated at or below 30 remaining cycles. Compare the prediction against known test labels.

Research status and limitations. NASA C-MAPSS FD001 is simulated engine degradation data. Our scored models are a classical CPU baseline and an original fixed-question temporal QA Transformer, not an OpenTime 5B fine-tune or a general-purpose language model. The separate OpenTime November 2026 5B pilot completed on Nebius and is documented above; the interactive selector is not the 5B model. The pilot's held-out F1 remains below the independent CPU-trained baselines. The demonstration must not be used for live aircraft maintenance or safety decisions.

Why this direction matters

An effective time-series AI employee should not merely predict a number. It should provide evidence, make errors visible, trigger human-review workflows, and help businesses take preventive action. This is our testable starting point toward that vision.
