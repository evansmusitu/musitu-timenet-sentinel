What this measures

  • A correct risk warning must happen while the official remaining life is at most 30 cycles.
  • A warning before that threshold is a premature false alarm under this exact evaluation policy.
  • 75 engines ended their recorded test histories before entering the final 30-cycle horizon; they cannot be counted as successful or failed near-failure detections.
  • Multiple windows from the same engine are correlated, so window F1 does not have an independent-sample interpretation.

Scientific honesty

The frozen November 20 checkpoint and final 100-engine prediction artifact must reproduce before any replay is scored. The model sees only past sensor observations and was neither retrained nor recalibrated for this experiment.

This is not the organizer OpenTime 5B checkpoint, a deployed maintenance alarm or real aviation safety validation. Source telemetry is not republished in the evidence file.
