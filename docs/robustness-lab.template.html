What happens when the data changes?

A strong model on a familiar test set can fail when the operating regime or fault mode changes. We tested two independently trained FD001 models against four official NASA C-MAPSS cohorts without target-cohort retraining, then explored training on three development cohorts to test the fourth.

What this means for a real product

Research-only limitation: FD004 compact warnings produced $compact_fd004_fp false alarms and $compact_fd004_fn missed at-risk engines. A demo should never be used for real aircraft operations. New operating regimes need validation, monitored alerts, human review and an explicit abstention policy before any deployment claim.

Projects tested on real-world turbine data address different assets and tasks. These NASA simulations cover a related engine family and do not establish cross-manufacturer generalization. Our results are not a direct leaderboard comparison.
